When I began to develop this thing, I had never intended for AMP to become a coding project. All I wanted was just to flip things around to match up the story that I had in mind.

Then like a year later, I realized that my mod had a ton of wasted potential: I felt like I was doing the bare minimum of what I could really do.
Most of the changes came when I was inputting my MacOS version "AMP T2E" maps as secret levels in Scenario 1. By that point in time I'd not put in the rocket launcher/flamethrower weapons.
It was really bugging me, as I was (and still am) a sucker for accuracy. So eventually what I did was put in some MacOS related things (like the faster pushwalls, the backpack, the colored key-doors, and some SoD walls)
That point on, I decided to do what most people fear a "retcon"... I began switching up the story as I realized more stuff than ever before.

I think it was ultimately for the better in the end, however... When you look at how version 1.0 came out with its sloppy maps, you'd be grateful I even changed stuff up.
